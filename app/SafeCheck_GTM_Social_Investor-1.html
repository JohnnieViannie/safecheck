Phase-by-Phase Launch Playbook

Mapped directly to your existing roadmap — with specific actions, channels, and success metrics per phase

Phase 1 · Months 1–2 · MVP Beta
50 Kampala Users — Proof of Concept
Target: 50 beta users across 5 target personas — community health workers, elderly users, construction supervisors, solo entrepreneurs, journalists.

Acquisition channels: Direct personal outreach via WhatsApp from founding team. Target community leaders, church networks, and professional associations in Kampala. Each team member personally recruits 10 users.

Success metrics: 80%+ of beta users complete onboarding and set up at least one trigger action. Daily check-in completion rate above 70%. Zero false triggers in beta period. Net Promoter Score above 50.

Key output: 5 documented user testimonials — one per persona — for use in Phase 2 press and marketing materials.
Phase 2 · Months 3–4 · Full Feature Launch
B2C Public Launch + First B2B Pilots
B2C launch channels: WhatsApp Status campaign (5 story cards per week). Instagram and Facebook organic content — story-led series featuring Grace, Ssekandi, Moses, Amina. Local press release distributed to Monitor, New Vision, Nile Post, and Kampala Sun.

B2B pilot targets: 3 construction or logistics companies in Uganda. Target safety managers directly via LinkedIn and professional networks. Offer a 3-month free Business Safety Plan trial. Pitch deck + one-pager ready for this phase.

Word-of-mouth mechanic: Every triggered alert is a new user acquisition event. Each contact who receives an alert sees the Safebangle brand and is directed to download the app. Ensure the trigger SMS includes a clear app download link.

Success metrics: 500 active B2C users. 2+ B2B pilots live. 3 press articles published. App Store rating above 4.2.
Phase 3 · Months 5–6 · Growth & Revenue
Telecoms Partnership + B2B Scale
Telecoms partnership pursuit: MTN Uganda, Airtel Africa, and Safaricom all have digital safety product lines. SafeCheck is a ready-made white-label or co-branded feature. The pitch: SafeCheck adds a life-safety safety layer to their data plans at zero engineering cost. Target partnership managers at MTN Uganda first — they have the most active digital product team in Uganda.

Family plan upsell activation: Every SafeCheck user who adds 3 contacts creates a family network. Prompt the user to invite contacts to download Safebangle directly — "Your contact will see your SafeCheck status in real time if they have the app." This is the family network flywheel.

PR campaign launch: "SafeCheck — Because Silence Shouldn't Mean the Worst." Pitch to BBC Africa, Al Jazeera Africa, and TechCabal. The human stories (Grace, Ssekandi, Amina) are the hook — not the technology.

Success metrics: 2,000+ active users. 1 telecoms partnership MOU signed. 5+ B2B accounts. Monthly Recurring Revenue above UGX 15M.
Phase 4 · Months 7–12 · Regional Expansion
Kenya, Tanzania, Nigeria — East and West Africa
Kenya first: The Nairobi-Kampala logistics corridor (Fatima's story) is a natural cross-border expansion case. Partner with a Kenyan logistics association. Swahili localisation ready from Phase 4 language work.

Nigeria second: The 30M+ smallholder farming households (Adaeze's story) are the B2C anchor. The B2B angle is agricultural cooperatives and microfinance institutions with field agents. Hausa and Yoruba localisation required.

Investor narrative: By Month 9 you have Uganda proof, Kenya traction, and Nigeria pipeline — the three largest economies in Sub-Saharan Africa. This is the Series A narrative. Full investor pitch deck (from Area 07 of this package) ready for this stage.

Success metrics: 10,000+ users across 3 markets. Series A raise process initiated. Telecoms partnership live in at least one market.
